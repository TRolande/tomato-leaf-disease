The four classes

What each class looks like on a leaf.

Healthy

Even green tissue with no spots, rings or fuzzy growth. Check the lower leaves once a week.

Early blight

Dark brown spots with concentric rings, usually on older lower leaves. Remove affected leaves and keep water off the foliage.

Late blight

Water-soaked dark patches that spread fast and turn brown or papery in cool, wet weather. Bag and remove infected leaves.

Leaf mold

Pale yellow patches on the upper surface, with olive-grey mould underneath, in humid air. Improve airflow.
